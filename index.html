01 / ABOUT ME

Curious about technology.
Driven by research.

I am an M.S. student in Engineering Technology at Middle Tennessee State University, where I also serve as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for Electronics.

I am a graduate student member of the Autonomous Systems and Quantum Intelligence (ASQI) Laboratory, directed by Dr. Mohammad Arif Hossain.

My academic background is rooted in antenna design, RF energy harvesting, rectenna systems, and wireless power transfer. I am interested in how these foundations connect to autonomous systems, integrated sensing and communication, and next-generation wireless networks.
